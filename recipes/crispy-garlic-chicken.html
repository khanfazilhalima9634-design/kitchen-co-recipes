Crispy Garlic Chicken

Golden crispy chicken finished with a rich garlic butter sauce. A fast weeknight dinner ready in about 30 minutes.

⏱ 30 min 👥 4 servings ⭐ 4.9

⏱ 30 minutes

👥 4 servings

📂 Chicken

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken thighs or breasts
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Fresh rosemary

How to Make It

  1. Season chicken with paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  2. Heat olive oil in a skillet.
  3. Cook chicken until deeply golden on both sides.
  4. Add butter and garlic.
  5. Spoon garlic butter over the chicken.
  6. Finish with rosemary and lemon juice.
  7. Serve immediately.

Tips for Success

  • Use skin-on chicken for extra crispiness.
  • Do not overcrowd the pan.
  • Rest chicken for 5 minutes before serving.

Recipe FAQ

Can I bake instead?

Yes, bake at 200°C for about 30 minutes.

Can I store leftovers?

Store refrigerated for up to 3 days.
